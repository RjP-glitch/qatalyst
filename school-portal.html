QATALYST School Portal · SDO Baliwag

Effective Date: January 1, 2026  ·  Schools Division Office of the City of Baliwag

1. Overview

By creating an account and using the QATALYST School Portal, you agree to be bound by these Terms of Service. This portal is operated by the Schools Division Office (SDO) of the City of Baliwag under the Department of Education (DepEd) and is intended solely for authorized school officials, teachers, and school administrators within the SDO Baliwag jurisdiction.

2. User Account Responsibilities

  • You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials. Do not share your password with anyone.
  • You must provide accurate and truthful information during registration, including your real name, school affiliation, district, and official email address.
  • Each user may only register one account. Multiple accounts for the same individual are not permitted.
  • You must notify SDO Baliwag immediately if you suspect unauthorized access to your account.
  • Accounts found to contain false information may be suspended or permanently deactivated without prior notice.

3. Document Submission Rules

  • All documents submitted through this portal must be official, complete, and duly signed by the appropriate school authority.
  • Submitted documents must be in the required file format (PDF, DOC, DOCX, or XLSX) and must not exceed the maximum file size limit.
  • Documents must correspond to the correct submission type. Submitting a document under the wrong category is the responsibility of the submitting user.
  • Submissions are subject to review by SDO Baliwag personnel. The portal does not guarantee immediate processing.
  • Once a document is submitted, it cannot be withdrawn. Users may re-submit a revised document only after receiving an indorsement notice.

4. SDO's Right to Endorse or Reject Documents

  • SDO Baliwag reserves the right to review, endorse, return, or reject any submitted document at its sole discretion.
  • A document may receive up to three (3) indorsements — each indicating that the document was returned with remarks and requires revision.
  • SDO Baliwag is not liable for delays in processing caused by incomplete or non-compliant submissions.
  • The indorsement count and status reflected in this portal are for reference only and do not constitute an official DepEd communication unless accompanied by a formal letter.

5. Prohibited Actions

  • Submitting falsified, forged, or fraudulent documents is strictly prohibited and may result in administrative and/or legal action.
  • Users must not attempt to gain unauthorized access to other accounts or to administrative functions of the portal.
  • Submitting documents on behalf of another school without proper authorization is not allowed.
  • Using the portal for any purpose other than its intended use is prohibited.

6. Consequences of Violations

Violations may result in suspension or permanent deactivation of the user's account, referral to the appropriate DepEd disciplinary authority, and/or filing of administrative charges in accordance with existing Civil Service and DepEd rules and regulations.

7. Changes to These Terms

SDO Baliwag reserves the right to update or modify these Terms of Service at any time. Continued use of the portal after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.

8. Contact

For questions regarding these Terms of Service, please contact SDO Baliwag or visit the Schools Division Office of the City of Baliwag.

Privacy Policy

QATALYST School Portal · SDO Baliwag

Effective Date: January 1, 2026  ·  In compliance with R.A. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012) and DepEd Order No. 72, s. 2016

1. Introduction

The Schools Division Office of the City of Baliwag is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the Republic Act No. 10173, otherwise known as the Data Privacy Act of 2012, and DepEd Order No. 72, s. 2016.

2. Information We Collect

  • Identity Information: Full name and School User account
  • Contact Information: Email address
  • School Information: School name, district, DepEd school ID, grade level or department
  • Account Data: Encrypted password, account status, email verification status
  • Submission Data: Uploaded documents, submission timestamps, indorsement history, and reviewer remarks
  • Technical Data: IP address and browser information for security logging purposes

3. Purpose of Data Collection

  • To create and manage your school portal account
  • To process and track document submissions
  • To facilitate SDO review and communication regarding submitted documents
  • To send email notifications regarding document status and verification
  • To maintain security logs and prevent unauthorized access
  • To generate official records and reports required by DepEd

4. Data Security

  • All passwords are encrypted using industry-standard bcrypt hashing and are never stored in plain text.
  • Session tokens are stored in HttpOnly cookies that are inaccessible to client-side scripts.
  • All data transmissions are conducted over HTTPS encrypted connections.
  • Access to your personal data is restricted to authorized SDO personnel only.

5. Data Sharing

Your personal data will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties. Information may only be disclosed to authorized SDO Baliwag personnel, DepEd regional or central offices when required by official directives, or law enforcement when required by law.

6. Data Retention

Your personal data will be retained for as long as your account is active or as required by DepEd records management policies. Upon account deactivation, personal data will be anonymized or securely deleted in accordance with applicable regulations.

7. Your Rights as a Data Subject

  • Be informed about how your data is collected and processed
  • Access your personal data held by SDO Baliwag
  • Correct inaccurate or outdated personal information
  • Object to the processing of your data on legitimate grounds
  • Erasure or blocking of your data when it is no longer necessary
  • File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (NPC)

8. Contact & Data Protection Officer

For privacy-related concerns, please contact:
SDO Baliwag — Data Protection Officer
Address: Schools Division Office of the City of Baliwag, Baliwag, Bulacan
