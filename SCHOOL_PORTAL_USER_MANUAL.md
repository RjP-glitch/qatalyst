# QATALYST School Portal User Manual

This guide covers tuition fee submissions and school calendar submissions in the school portal.

## Tuition Fee Submission

### Create or edit a draft

1. Open **School Tuition Fee** and choose **Create New Tuition Fee Document**, or open an existing draft with **Open Builder**.
2. Choose whether the document requests a fee increase or reports no increase, then select the school levels included.
3. Complete the school information and choose the **Current SY**. The **Proposed SY** is kept one year after the current year; changing either year updates the other.
4. Enter the approved current tuition and proposed tuition by grade, plus any applicable miscellaneous or other fees. Review the calculated increases and totals.
5. In **Signatories**, use **Load** to fill saved names. The school principal details are school-managed. SDO or regional office signatories are maintained by SDO personnel and are read-only in the school builder.
6. Use **Save Draft** while work is in progress. Move between sections, including **Review**, from the builder navigation.
7. Use **Show Preview** to review the document or **Export to Word** to download a Word copy.

### Tuition baseline check

When an accepted fee schedule exists for the selected current school year, the amounts entered as current tuition are checked against that schedule at submission. If they do not match, the system blocks submission and identifies that the baseline needs to be checked with the SDO. Correct the current amounts to match the accepted schedule, or contact the SDO if the stored baseline is missing or needs correction. A school cannot create or change the accepted baseline itself.

After the SDO approves or releases an accepted tuition submission, its proposed fees become the baseline for that school year. This check helps keep later submissions consistent with the accepted schedule.

### Submit or resubmit

- Select **Final Submit** in the builder actions and confirm the submission. A submitted document becomes read-only while it is under review.
- If the SDO returns a document for correction, open it, review the SDO feedback, make corrections, and choose **Resubmit**.
- If a document is already submitted for final approval, use **Request Edit Approval**. Wait for the SDO to approve the request before editing and resubmitting.
- Track submission status in **My Submissions** or **Document Tracker**.

## School Calendar Submission

1. Open **School Calendar** and create a new calendar or continue a saved draft.
2. Complete the school information, school year, opening and closing dates, calendar markings, remarks, and signatories.
3. Use **Save & Continue** to save progress between sections. Review the calendar preview and confirm that the school-day total meets the builder's requirement before final submission.
4. Choose **Final Submit** and confirm. Submitted calendars are read-only unless the SDO approves an edit request.
5. If the calendar is returned for correction, follow the SDO feedback and resubmit it.

## Getting help

If a tuition baseline is unavailable or incorrect, contact the SDO office before submitting. For an approved edit request or a returned document, follow the status and feedback shown in the portal.
