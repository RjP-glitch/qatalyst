Manage the signatories used by the School Calendar and Tuition Fee builders. Each role belongs to one builder (or both, if marked "Shared") — the group headings below show which. Scope Builder Role Name Position Office Actions
Select a scope and school to load signatories.
School-owned rows are used by private schools. Office-managed rows are for SDO/SMME-only signatories such as Regional Director and Schools Division Superintendent (used for the "Thru" line on transmittal/LOI letters). "Processed and Evaluated By", "Validated By", and "Noted By" are Tuition Fee builder-only roles — they will not appear anywhere in the School Calendar builder. "Validated By" and "Noted By" are office-managed only (e.g., EPS/QAD and the Regional Director/CESO signatory on the tuition fee report) and won't show under the School-owned scope.
